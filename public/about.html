Who We Are

From the Field to Your Kitchen — with Zero Compromise

Atalanta Food is a Lagos-based agricultural produce sales and distribution company built on one principle: fresh produce should go from farm to customer as fast as possible, with no needless middlemen inflating cost or degrading quality.

Our name, Atá lán ta, is Yoruba for "It is pepper we sell" — a proud declaration of origin. While pepper was where we started, Atalanta today sources over eleven premium Nigerian produce lines: habanero peppers, bell peppers, fresh ugu leaves, Jos plum tomatoes, Benue white yam tubers, golden sweet corn, crisp cucumbers, tender okra, soursop, and buttery avocados.

Whether you're a household stocking your kitchen or a hotel, restaurant, or catering operation needing reliable commercial volumes — Atalanta is your single point of contact for verified, inspected, farm-fresh Nigerian produce.

How It Works

Farm Partnership & Sourcing

We work directly with Nigerian farm cooperatives and smallholder farms across Benue, Jos, and the South-West agricultural belt to source produce at peak freshness.

Field Inspection & Grading

Every batch is physically inspected before packaging. We grade by size, ripeness, and quality — rejecting anything that doesn't meet our commercial standards.

Rapid Dispatch to Your Door

Same-day or next-morning dispatch for retail and commercial orders. WhatsApp-driven ordering means no delays — just fast, fresh delivery.
